Scan model exploration

Exploration only. No production scan classes or live game state changed.

Reproduce
  php experiments/scan-models/run.php 1000
  Historical mock-resolver checks have been superseded by Simulation/WorkScanTest.php.

Method
  3 models x 4 distance/leg fixtures x 3 capability factors x 1,000 seeded trials.
  36,000 independent model/fixture/capability runs; three speed/cost modes reuse
  those cycle counts for 108 reported configurations. Common random seeds across
  models and equipment; deterministic reruns. No trial hit the 10,000-cycle cap.
  Nine additional cases run candidate resolvers through the actual in-memory
  action factory, action processor, and checkpoint clock, compared to the same
  standalone model. These are lifecycle checks, not full production scan logic.

Assumptions
  Reference expected cycles = 12 * (1 + distance_ly^1.5) * difficulty / capability.
  Difficulty is 1 for near/Lacaille and 1.3 for long fixtures.
  Capability factors 0.7, 1, 1.5 are hypothetical, not mapped to catalog equipment.
  Near = 0.5 ly; Lacaille = 2.416057 ly; long = 6.5 ly.
  Three-leg fixture splits the same 6.5 ly into three equal legs.
  Economy: 600 seconds and 1 core per attempt.
  Normal: 300 seconds and 2 core per attempt.
  Fast: 150 seconds and 4 core per attempt.
  Costs are illustrative accounting, not inventory deductions or tuned prices.
  Resource exhaustion is reported against a 631-core budget but does not stop
  trials. No baseline core decay, capacitor, or power regeneration is simulated.
  Topology is supplied as fixtures, not generated from the actual star map.
  No waypoint continuation-stop roll; trials keep working toward completion.

Models
  fixed: independent success probability 1 / expectedCycles each attempt.
  rising: Weibull shape 2 conditional discovery probability after each miss,
    scaled to the same continuous mean; discretization adds roughly half a cycle.
  work: each attempt has 75% chance to gain one unit of work, requiring
    ceil(0.75 * expectedCycles) units. No second discovery roll.

Interpretation
  Accumulated work gives the clearest progress-bar meaning and predictable
  completion windows while retaining occasional unsuccessful cycles.
  Fixed discovery rolls produce both first-cycle completions and very long tails.
  Increasing odds reduces those extremes but still permits long outliers and
  does not represent completed work.
  Fast mode halves elapsed time and doubles total cost relative to normal.
  The provisional prices are too expensive for long fast scans with 631 core.
  Basic ships and economy settings exceed a day under this calibration.
  Near scans average 1.44h, below a strict two-hour minimum; the fixed workload
  term would need increasing if even nearby scans should take several hours.
  Convex distance scaling makes three shorter legs cheaper than one long leg
  of equal total length: 16.65h versus 22.88h in the work model. Decide whether
  this is intended before applying the formula per waypoint.
  At 24h roughly 288 five-minute attempts accumulate; full per-cycle history
  should be reviewed for storage and serialization cost.
  Existing Power already models a regenerating tactical resource. Examine its
  intended relationship to scanning before adding a separate capacitor.

Suggested next experiment
  Tune a pure scan-work model shared by simulation and a future action resolver.
  Separate difficulty, work gain, cycle duration, and cost. Compare total scan
  budget versus per-leg budgets, and enforce resource exhaustion explicitly.
  Keep equipment modifiers distinct from player-selected intensity.
